Perspectives

Thoughts on how sound research takes shape

The essays gathered here are perspectives rather than prescriptions. Each one reflects on a part of the research process we consider important, from defining a well-framed question to verifying information and reporting what it shows. They are offered as a candid look at the thinking behind our approach.
