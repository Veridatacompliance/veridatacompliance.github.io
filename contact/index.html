Contact

Begin a conversation with VCRG by email

Write to us at info@veridatacompliance.org to share what you are working on, and we aim to follow up and explore how we might be of help.

Email

Start a conversation

Email is the preferred way to reach us, at info@veridatacompliance.org. A brief note describing your question and its context is a practical place to begin, and we can explore the details together from there.

Email info@veridatacompliance.org

Before you write

What to include in your first email

A few details can help us understand your question from the start. None are required, and we ask that you describe any material in general terms rather than sending sensitive personal information.

  • A short description of the question you would like researched
  • Any background or context that may help frame the work
  • A general description of any information or data you already have
  • The form you would prefer for findings, such as a summary or report
  • Any timing considerations or dates you are working toward

What happens next

What happens after you write

Once your email arrives, our approach follows a simple, considered path toward understanding your needs. The steps below describe what you can generally expect.

  1. 01

    We read and review

    We read your message attentively to understand the question, the context around it, and what a helpful outcome might look like for you.

  2. 02

    We follow up

    We intend to reply by email, and our response may include questions to refine scope, priorities, or the form of findings that would serve you well.

  3. 03

    We outline a path

    Where there is a fit, we describe a proposed scope and way of working together, so you can decide whether and how to move forward.

Questions

Common questions

If your question isn't answered here, please contact us by email.

What kind of work does VCRG take on?

We focus on gathering, organizing, verifying, and interpreting information, and on reporting findings in a practical, readable format. The scope of each engagement is defined by the specific questions involved.

Who does VCRG work with?

VCRG seeks to work with business clients. Our work is intended to be tailored to each client's particular questions and context.

How does an engagement get started?

Reach out by email with a short outline of what you need. We aim to review it, follow up with any questions, and, where there is a fit, propose a scope before any work begins.

What form do findings take?

Findings can be presented as a concise written summary, a more detailed report, or an organized set of materials, depending on what the engagement calls for and how the information will be used.

Can the scope change as the work develops?

Yes. If priorities shift or new questions emerge, we can discuss adjusting the scope together and agree on any changes before proceeding.

Is VCRG a government agency?

No. VCRG is a private research firm and is not affiliated with any government agency.

Do you provide legal or financial advice?

No. VCRG provides research services only. We do not provide legal, financial, or investment advice.

How is information on these pages handled?

These informational pages do not use cookies or analytics, and we do not sell personal information collected through them. Details are in our privacy notice.
