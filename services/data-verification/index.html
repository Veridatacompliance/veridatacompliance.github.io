Services

Data Verification and Quality Review

We take a deliberate, methodical look at the information you already hold, checking it for consistency and accuracy and noting where attention may be needed.

Overview

A closer look at the information you rely on

Information tends to pass through many hands before it reaches a decision. Along the way, entries can drift out of date, formats can diverge, and small inconsistencies can accumulate. Our Data Verification and Quality Review service is intended to bring a steady, attentive eye to that information so that questions can be identified and addressed in context.

This service differs from original research in that it starts with material you provide. We focus on comparing, cross-checking, and reviewing what is already there, then describing what we observe in straightforward language. Our intent is not to replace your judgment but to offer a more precise view of the condition of your information.

Scope

What the service can include

Engagements are shaped around the material and the questions at hand. Depending on scope, the work can include elements such as these.

  • Consistency checks across related fields and entries
  • Comparison of data against reference material you supply
  • Identification of duplicates, gaps, and conflicting values
  • Review of formatting, labeling, and naming conventions
  • Focused review of selected portions of a data set
  • Written summaries of observations and open questions

How we work

How we approach verification work

This service draws on the same principles that guide all of our work, applied here to the particular demands of checking information.

  1. 01

    Agreed criteria

    At the outset, we seek to work with you to define what accurate and consistent mean for your material, so the review reflects your standards rather than assumptions of our own.

  2. 02

    Methodical checking

    We intend to work through information in a consistent, orderly way, keeping track of what has been reviewed and how, so that our observations can be followed and revisited later.

  3. 03

    Precise, measured findings

    We report what we observe in straightforward terms, distinguishing confirmed inconsistencies from open questions, and leave decisions about corrections with you.

Questions

Questions about data verification

What kind of information can you review?

Engagements can involve many kinds of business information, from structured data sets to working files and reference material. We are glad to discuss your material by email and describe whether and how this service might apply.

Do you correct the data, or only review it?

Our focus is on reviewing and reporting. We describe what we find and where questions remain, and decisions about changes stay with you. If additional support is helpful, the scope of an engagement can be discussed and adjusted.

How is this service different from Research and Analysis?

Research and Analysis generally involves gathering and examining information to address a question. Data Verification and Quality Review starts with information you already have and looks closely at its consistency and accuracy. The services can also be combined.
