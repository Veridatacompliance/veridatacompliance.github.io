Services

Information Organization and Reporting

We help business clients bring order to the information they hold, arranging it into readable summaries and reports that are straightforward to reference and share.

Overview

From scattered information to orderly reporting

Information often arrives in many forms and from many directions, which can make it difficult to see the whole picture. Our focus with this service is on bringing that material together, sorting it in a logical way, and presenting it so that the points that matter are easy to find and understand.

Reporting is where research becomes useful to the people who rely on it. We seek to write in direct, straightforward language, to separate findings from assumptions, and to shape each report around the questions a client has asked, so that it can support discussion, planning, and informed decisions.

Scope

What this service can include

Engagements can be shaped around the material at hand and the kind of output that suits each client's needs and audience.

  • Consolidation of information from multiple sources
  • Logical categorization and indexing of material
  • Written summaries of key findings
  • Readable, well-organized narrative reports
  • Tables and reference lists for convenient review
  • Periodic status updates during an engagement

How we work

How we approach organization and reporting

Our approach to this service draws on the principles that guide all of our work, applied to the task of making information orderly and workable.

  1. 01

    Clarity of structure

    We look to arrange material in a logical order that reflects how it will be read and used, with consistent headings and categories that help readers move through it with ease.

  2. 02

    Readable writing

    We intend our reports to be read easily by a range of audiences, favoring direct sentences, defined terms, and summaries that bring the central points forward.

  3. 03

    Traceable findings

    Where it is helpful, we seek to note the source behind each finding and to distinguish between what was observed, what was inferred, and what remains open for further review.

Questions

Questions about organizing and reporting

What kind of information can you help organize?

Engagements can involve a wide range of business information, from notes and spreadsheets to collected research material. We discuss the material and its purpose at the outset so that the scope can reflect the client's needs.

What format will the final report take?

The format is shaped around how the report will be used. Engagements can include written narrative reports, concise summaries, tables, or a combination, agreed with the client before work begins.

Can this service be combined with other research work?

Yes. Organization and reporting can stand alone or follow on from research and verification work. We aim to fit this service into a wider engagement in a way that suits the client's goals.
