Ongoing Research Support

A continuing research arrangement for business clients whose questions do not end with a defined project, intended to build familiarity and consistency over time.

Overview

Research support that continues with you

Some information needs are not tied to a defined end point. They return on a regular basis, shift as priorities change, or grow as a business takes on new questions. Ongoing Research Support is intended for these situations, offering a continuing arrangement in which we gather, organize, and review information as needs arise rather than starting fresh each time.

Over the course of an engagement, we aim to build a working understanding of your context, preferred formats, and recurring questions. That familiarity can make each new request easier to scope and each update more consistent with what came before, so the research you receive reads as part of a coherent body of work.

Scope

What ongoing support can include

Each arrangement is shaped around the client. Depending on needs, ongoing support can draw on elements such as the following.

  • Recurring research on questions you return to
  • Periodic updates to earlier findings
  • Ad hoc requests handled within an agreed scope
  • Continuity of format and terminology across reports
  • Ongoing organization of accumulated information
  • Regular check-ins to revisit priorities and scope

How we work

How ongoing support works

Ongoing support is meant to be steady and adaptable. These practices describe how we seek to keep a continuing engagement orderly and practical.

  1. 01

    A shared starting point

    We begin by learning about your context, the questions that tend to recur, and how you prefer findings to be presented, so later work can build on a common foundation.

  2. 02

    A rhythm that fits

    Requests can follow a regular cadence, arrive as needs come up, or blend these approaches. We look to work within whatever pattern suits your business and to keep each request well defined.

  3. 03

    Periodic review of scope

    As priorities shift, we revisit the scope of support together, adjusting focus, depth, or format so the arrangement can stay aligned with what your business needs at that point.

Questions

Questions about research support

How is ongoing support different from a defined project?

A defined project centers on a specific question with a set scope. Ongoing support is a continuing arrangement in which requests can arise over time. As the work progresses, we seek to develop familiarity with your context so that related requests stay consistent.

Can the scope of ongoing support change over time?

Yes. Ongoing arrangements are meant to be flexible. Focus, depth, and format can be revisited as your priorities evolve, and we intend to discuss and agree any adjustments with you before they take effect.

How does an ongoing support arrangement begin?

It starts with an email to info@veridatacompliance.org describing your general needs. From there, we aim to follow up by email to understand your context and outline a scope that fits how you work.
