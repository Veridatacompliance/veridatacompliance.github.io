Careers

Work grounded in rigor, clarity, and curiosity

We value people who take pride in methodical work, think precisely about information, and treat client matters with tact and respect.

Working at VCRG

The nature of our work

Our work centers on gathering, organizing, verifying, and analyzing information so that clients can approach their decisions with greater confidence. It calls for patience, attention to detail, and a willingness to look closely at what the information shows rather than what one might expect it to show.

Engagements can include defined projects, ongoing support, and work shaped around a particular question. In each case, we seek to apply the same principles of accuracy, discretion, and responsiveness, and we value people who share them.

What we value

Qualities we value

These are the personal traits we see as supporting methodical, considered research and thoughtful collaboration with clients.

  • Attention to detail

    We appreciate people who catch small inconsistencies, take the time to check their work, and look to get the particulars right before moving on.

  • Intellectual honesty

    We value people who follow the evidence wherever it points, distinguish what is known from what is not, and present findings directly and without embellishment.

  • Clear communication

    We value people who write and speak with clarity, choose their words with precision, and organize ideas in a way that readers and listeners can readily follow.

  • Discretion and reliability

    We value people who handle information with tact and discretion, follow through on commitments, and respect the time of those they work with.

Future opportunities

Expressing interest

If you would like to be considered for future opportunities, you are welcome to send a brief introduction to info@veridatacompliance.org at your convenience. A short note describing your background, the kind of work that interests you, and why VCRG appeals to you is a helpful place to begin. We appreciate the time it takes to write, and we may be in touch if a suitable opportunity arises.
