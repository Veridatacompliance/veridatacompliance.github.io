Website Privacy Notice

This page describes, in straightforward terms, what information may be collected when you visit the informational pages of our website and how it is handled.

Last updated November 18, 2026

What this page covers

This privacy page covers the informational pages of veridatacompliance.org. Some services may be offered through separate pages that collect information. Those pages provide their own privacy statements.

No cookies, analytics, or forms

These informational pages do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers, and they do not contain forms.

Website hosting

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When a page is requested, GitHub may collect technical information, such as IP addresses, for security and operation purposes, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Fonts

Fonts on this website are loaded from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests font files from Google, which receives standard technical information such as your IP address, as described in Google's privacy policy.

When you email us

If you email info@veridatacompliance.org, we receive the information you include and use it to respond, to follow up on your inquiry or introduction, and to carry out any work you request.

Sale of personal information

VCRG does not sell personal information collected through these informational pages.

Links to other websites

Pages on this website may link to other websites, which are governed by their own privacy policies rather than by this page.

Intended audience

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children.

Updates to this page

This page may be revised periodically. The date shown on this page reflects the latest version.

Questions

If you have questions about this page, please write to info@veridatacompliance.org.
